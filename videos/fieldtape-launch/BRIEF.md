## Intent

Launch FieldTape for Kaggriculture as an unofficial, genuinely playable quant-learning game. Market the product with its own real screens: begin with the constraint, prove the game loop, reveal the market and risk lessons, then send viewers to play and to the official Kaggle competition.

The tone is sharp, curious, and editorial rather than corporate. The viewer should feel that a cozy farming game has unexpectedly become a capital-allocation problem.
